To continue exploring the wide range of emotional experiences, here are further examples, including poetic concepts, interpersonal nuances, and intense states:
Examples include:
Vellichor: The wistful atmosphere of a used bookshop.
Nodus tollens: The sense that the direction of one's life no longer makes sense.
Chrysalism: The calm felt indoors during a thunderstorm.
Jouska: Mentally rehearsing hypothetical conversations.
Kenopsia: The desolate feeling of a usually busy place now empty.
Opia: The intense feeling of direct eye contact with someone.
Énouement: The wistful feeling of having reached the future but unable to inform one's past self.
L'appel du vide: A French term for the sudden urge to jump from high places.
Rubatosis: Heightened awareness of one's own heartbeat.
Alexithymia: Difficulty identifying or articulating one's own feelings.
Desiderium: A feeling of longing or loss for something that is gone.
Altschmerz: Weariness with persistent, long-standing problems.
Pâro: The feeling that one's actions will inevitably turn out poorly.
Gwenders: The tingling sensation from very cold fingers.
Malneirophrenia: Unhappiness upon waking from a bad dream.
Euneirophrenia: Satisfaction upon waking from a pleasant dream.
Morass: Feeling trapped or confused in a difficult situation.
Brumous: A dreary feeling, like a foggy winter day.
Dormiveglia: The state between sleeping and waking.
Bettschwere: German for the desire to stay in bed.
Clinomania: An excessive wish to remain in bed.
Yoin: A Japanese term for the lingering sensation of a past experience.
Sehnsucht: A deep, intense German yearning for something distant or indefinable.
Gjensynsglede: Norwegian for the joy of reuniting with someone after a long time.
Koi No Yokan: Japanese for the feeling upon meeting someone that you are destined to fall in love.
La douleur exquise: French for the exquisite sadness of unrequited love.
Fremdschämen: German for vicarious embarrassment felt for another person.
Pena ajena: Spanish, similar to Fremdschämen.
Gigil: A Filipino urge to pinch something cute.
Abundamort: Feeling overwhelmed by intense love to the point of feeling like dying.
Lenniness: An intense desire to hug a small, cute creature.
Hupokringesis: The discomfort of judging someone online for actions one might also commit.
Instagrief: Sadness over missing a moment while trying to capture it for social media.
Sonder: The realization that every person encountered has a life as complex as one's own.
Monachopsis: A subtle, persistent feeling of not belonging.
Exulansis: The reluctance to discuss an experience because others won't understand.
Adronitis: Frustration with the slow process of getting to know someone.
Pretedium: Annoyance and boredom when stuck listening to someone known for long monologues.
Force Majeure: Vague humiliation resulting from an instinctive flight response.
Confusional: Momentary disorientation suggesting possible delusion.
Cine-void: Guilt about choosing a familiar movie over a critically acclaimed one.
Disenchantment: A state of being disappointed after expecting something to be better.
Disillusionment: Realization that something is not as good as one believed.
Lypophrenia: A Greek term for vague sadness without a clear cause.
Toska: A Russian sensation of deep spiritual anguish without a specific reason.
Nadryv: A Russian term for an uncontrolled emotional outburst.
Zugzwang: A German term for a situation where any move made will worsen one's position.
Schwarmerei: German for excessive or unrestrained enthusiasm or sentiment.
Dacrygelos: Greek for alternating laughing and crying in a tragic situation.
Litost: A Czech mix of shame, resentment, and fury from feeling wretched due to someone else.
Torschlusspanik: German for "gate-closing panic," anxiety over diminishing opportunities with age.
Morkkis: A Finnish term for the moral or psychological hangover after drunken behavior.
Verklempt: Yiddish/German for being choked with emotion.
Rocambolesco: Italian for a spirit of adventurous swagger.
Anker: A feeling of being compelled to resist or fight when cornered.
Creatiphobia: Anxiety about one's ability to create something new.
Creatiphoria: The strong conviction that something one is creating is brilliant.
